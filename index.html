What Does "Scan Here" on a QR Code Mean?

"Please scan here" is a prompt printed next to a QR code. It tells you to open your phone camera, point it at the code, and tap the link that appears. With QR Here you can do the same from your browser, with no app. Here are the most common prompts and what they open:

  • Scan here to pay: Opens a payment page or wallet. Check the link carefully before you pay.
  • Scan here to learn more: Opens a website, menu, brochure, or product page.
  • Scan here for location: Opens a map pin with directions.
  • Scan here to join: Connects you to a Wi-Fi network, meeting, or event.
  • Scan here to contact us: Opens WhatsApp, a phone call, SMS, email, or a contact card.

Need a Wi-Fi QR code scanner? Scan a Wi-Fi code to see the network name and password. Need a WhatsApp QR code scanner? Scan the code to see the chat link before you open it. Both work with your camera or an image upload.
